MODEL DECISIONS
4 mL/g is retained as a supplied practical starting assumption, not falsely attributed to a manufacturer.
135/180 g recipe comes from rounding each exact label conversion UP to a 5 g weigh-out; mixed batch sums the rounded rows.
12-cup near-rim preset and 13.5-cup comparison preserve earlier discussion without treating it as a crop optimum.
Water +/-20% display is sensitivity only, not a statistical interval or prescribed final range.
Calibration requires dry sample mass + expanded volume + a damp/no-drip observation. Optional retained water calibrates water separately.
No daily-irrigation, nutrient dose, shelf life or harvest forecast is computed.
