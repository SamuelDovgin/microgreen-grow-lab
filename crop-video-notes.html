Creator transcripts: density, water and protocol differences

Research date: November 1, 2026. This is a targeted caption review, not a review of every creator video online.

Coverage and method

The existing archive indexes 87 Microgreen Manager videos, 183 Grow With Jonah recordings and 79 Donny Greens recordings, plus Shorts and articles. Those are discovery catalogs; their old quality reports correctly say zero full transcripts reviewed. This pass retrieved caption tracks for 17 selected recordings across four creator groups, adding On The Grow’s crop demonstrations and comparisons. It inspected relevant timestamped passages, crop/tray context, and spoken numeric settings. It does not retroactively relabel the older catalogs as transcript-reviewed.

The selection covers a whole-cycle cabbage example; broccoli on reusable medium; sunflower, pea, radish and cilantro demonstrations; substrate comparisons; basil/cilantro/sunflower advice; and commercial soaking/watering routines. Auto-captions can misrecognize quantities and plant names. Numeric values below were read with surrounding context and compared with creator articles where available, but the recordings were not visually verified in this pass. No claim of exhaustive YouTube coverage is made.

What the comparisons change

  • Default by species and system, not a creator average. On The Grow’s split cilantro demonstration says 10 g, Jonah says 20 g, and MGM says 40 g. A three-way average is not a new validated recipe. [V06,V10,D18]
  • Root zone matters. The reusable-medium broccoli demo checks surface moisture twice daily; the pea white-tray demo has an unnormalized kit area. Neither provides a general mL-per-1020 rule. [V02,V04]
  • Separate gross and usable output. A sunflower media comparison discards a crop after recording mass. The optimizer accepts zero saleable output for failed trays rather than deleting failures. [V08]
  • Germination cover and stretch blackout are separate. Donny’s general workflow uses a development cue; On The Grow sometimes adds a noncontact dark stage; MGM generally goes directly to light. [V01,V02,V14,D04]
  • Soak windows differ. Donny’s sunflower walkthrough specifies 4 h; USU specifies 12 h. Neither establishes a universal optimal soaking duration, and soaking is not sanitation. [V13,D03]
  • Water by condition. MGM demonstrates weight differences and teaches feedback from the previous watering round. A 500-mL example in a Jonah clip is conditional, not a dose to put into every calendar row. [V10,V17]
