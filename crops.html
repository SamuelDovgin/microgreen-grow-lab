How to turn the example into your protocol

  1. Water evenly, allow drainage, then weigh the complete tray with the same trays and accessories each time.
  2. Weigh again after a known interval. Account for water added and drained; avoid comparing a wet reservoir with a drained tray.
  3. Calculate net loss per area and time. Replace the demand example and set a conservative refill trigger.
  4. Check the seed surface separately during germination. Recalibrate for growth, new substrate, climate and canopy changes.

Refill ≈ max(0, drained target mass − current mass) / water density
Loss (mm/day) ≈ (start mass + water added − drainage − end mass) / area(m²) / 1000 / elapsed days

Plant growth and changing tray contents bias mass-based water estimates. A moisture buffer represents water storage, not a guarantee that all pores remain aerated. Water-model limits ↗

Broccoli starter · leave the 15 lb paver off. Rest an empty cover tray on the seeds and set Added top load to 0 lb. The calendar shows any load you select until cover removal. Check around day 3 for an evenly emerged stand that is anchored and starting to lift the tray. The starter then uses one day with the cover raised above the shoots in darkness.
