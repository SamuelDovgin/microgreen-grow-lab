These were reviewed in the existing archive; this pass reuses that attribution rather than claiming to have re-watched every video.

It separately traces a **Barrina T8, 4 ft, 42 W pinkish-white LED** option in [a later description](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eBVnA_Rxak4).

The project comparison records an earlier **12 h on / 12 off** T5 example. This is a historical practice example, not a universal schedule or a verified current whole-farm count. No bars-per-shelf preset is fabricated for Donny.

The shared T5 label cannot make a 54 W high-output fluorescent equivalent to a 20–22 W LED shop bar. Heat, optics, spectrum, efficacy and replacement costs differ; measure the canopy and electricity before comparing results.

## Compare dose, not just hours

At the same **illustrative 200 PPFD**, a 12 h day gives **8.64 DLI**, 16 h gives **11.52**, and 18 h gives **12.96**. These are unit conversions, not proof that the fixtures, crop response, yield or energy efficiency match.

For a useful shelf comparison, hold crop/cultivar, lot, medium, fill, seed density and water consistent; record PPFD across the canopy, spectrum, geometry, actual draw and climate. Compare saleable grams, stem form, rejected mass and light kWh. Repeated batches are more informative than a single visually good tray.
