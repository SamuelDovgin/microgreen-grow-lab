[D63] Truzzi et al. 2021 · soybean microgreen cultivation
URL: https://doi.org/10.3390/foods10081690
Accessed: 2026-11-06
Class: Controlled study
Used in: bean protocols, density/soak comparison, planner source trail.
Supports / key record: Foods 10 (2021), 1690; DOI 10.3390/foods10081690; methods §2.1. Five species; soybean 25 seeds/8-cm pot (pea alone has 10-cm pots), 24 h soak, peat/clay 80:20, 24 h dark at 18°C, then 16/8 h light/dark; five pots per light/species/time combination; final day 10.
