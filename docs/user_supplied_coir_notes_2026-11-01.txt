3. Fill your trays

Spread the moistened coir evenly in your shallow trays with drainage holes. Lightly level the surface without heavily compressing it.

What about storage?
Remaining dry brick: Keep somewhere cool and dry, preferably in a clean container or bag protected from humidity.

Unused hydrated coir: Store in a clean, loosely covered container for short-term use. Don't leave wet coir sealed away for extended periods.

Longer-term leftovers: Let unused clean coir dry completely before storing it dry. Discard any that develop mold or an unpleasant odor.

The microgreen guide at RusticWise specifically recommends mixing only what you need, although it notes that properly stored hydrated coir can last longer.

For your setup: I'd hydrate enough for just two trays at a time (about 4 quarts expanded), rather than all four. You'll have less material sitting around damp, and you can adjust your coir depth and moisture after your first couple of grows.
