Greenhouse measurement guidelines (PDF)

Lesser-known tips & tricks
Source notebook · November 15, 2026

The guide applies research and firsthand practices to shallow 1020 trays. The
suggested measurements are local checks. No tray-level improvement has been
measured for this guide. Relative archive paths below start at research_project/.

[T01] User observation and sunflower screenshots
Access: 2026-11-15; user text and two attachments. Class: anecdote.
Used in: corner anchoring and sunflower screenshot note.
Record: user observed corner-only lodging as a reason to check for limited
substrate. Two screenshots show a sunflower grower and commenters discussing
visible mold and home sanitizer mixtures. They do not establish the cause of
the pictured growth, a seed treatment, or the outcome of a paired tray test.
Preservation: paraphrase here; originals stay with the user’s attachments.
Status: source video and comment thread were not independently identified.

[T02] Grow Lab coir guide and existing research
Access: 2026-11-15; local archive. Used in: tray and corner checks, overlap review.
Record: the coir guide already asks for a center and four corner depth readings.
The cultivation and irrigation guides cover dry corners, tray level, uneven
light, and damping-off. The seed guide covers vigor, and the experiment and
economics guides already use saleable weight and labor efficiency. The tips
apply them through before/after measurements, marked orientation, cross-swaps,
visible quality checks and side-by-side throughput tests.

[T03] UMass Greenhouse Best Management Practices, growing media
URL: https://www.umass.edu/agriculture-food-environment/book/export/html/9854
Access: 2026-11-15; live. Class: extension. Used in: corners and wetting plugs.
Record: mix, container height, handling and watering influence air and water
in media; compaction can limit air space. Corner anchoring and local comparisons
are an inference. The manual has no microgreen corner intervention yield data.
Preservation: URL and support notes; no full copy redistributed.

[T04] UMN Extension, How to prevent seedling damping off
URL: https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/yard-and-garden-problems/how-to-prevent-seedling-damping-off
Access: 2026-11-15; live. Class: extension.
Used in: the first card and sunflower note.
Record: thinning and water-soaking at the stem base, decay and tissue collapse
are diagnostic clues. General seedling guidance rather than a food-safety
test. The local response is documented in
05_cultivation_workflows/02_mold_disease_pest_diagnostics.md.
Preservation: URL and support notes; no full copy redistributed.

[T05] Virginia Tech Extension, Introduction to Microgreen Production in Indoor
Vertical Farms and Greenhouses, SPES-756
URL: https://www.pubs.ext.vt.edu/content/dam/pubs_ext_vt_edu/spes/spes-756/SPES-756.pdf
Access: 2026-11-15; live. Class: extension. Used in: orientation, sowing and release.
Record: describes weighed rates, hand distribution and shaker sowing,

Orientation and photo-based comparisons are local adaptations.
Preservation: source_docs/extension/virginia-tech-microgreen-production-SPES-756.pdf.

[T06] UF/IFAS, Measuring Uniformity of Water Application of Sprinkler Systems,
AE194, Haman and Yeager
URL: https://edis.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/AE194/pdf
Access: 2026-11-15; publication PDF retrieved. Class: extension methods.
Used in: loaded shelf and catch cups.
Record: compare equal collectors across locations to identify uneven delivery.
The authors evaluate nursery sprinkler irrigation. Hand passes and shallow
reservoir water depth are adaptations; omit nursery acceptance thresholds.
Preservation: URL and support notes; no full copy redistributed.

[T07] Paperpot / Scintilla Farms, Arugula Microgreens in Paperpot Trays and
Microgreens Growing Guide
URLs: https://paperpot.co/how-to-grow-arugula-microgreens-in-paperpot-trays/
https://paperpot.co/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Microgreens-Growing-Guide.pdf
Access: 2026-11-15; live. Class: firsthand farm practice via supplier.
Used in: cover fit, blade height and direct packing.
Record: tray geometry and headspace differ across products. The farm describes
sharp knife cuts above the medium and direct packing. The check of rocking,
the cut-height comparison and the timing trial were developed for this guide.
Commercial relationship and no controlled comparison for these tests. Only
harvest and tray-fit observations were used.
Preservation: URL and support notes; no full copy redistributed.

[T08] Purdue Extension, Evaluating Container Substrates and Their Components,
HO-255-W
URL: https://www.extension.purdue.edu/extmedia/HO/HO-255-W.pdf
Access: 2026-11-15; live. Class: extension. Used in: settled depth.
Record: discusses settling, air space and container capacity as functions of
material and geometry. The before/after bed test uses actual grow trays.
Preservation: URL and support notes; no full copy redistributed.

[T09] Johnny’s Selected Seeds, Yield Data Trial for 29 Microgreen Varieties
URL: https://prod-na02.johnnyseeds.com/growers-library/vegetables/microgreens/micro-greens-yield-data-trial-summary-discussion.html
Access: 2026-11-15; live. Class: supplier’s firsthand trial.
Used in: shaker mass, early/late trays, harvest-delay test and separate blends.
Record: at least three sowings per variety in 1020 flats. Weighed packet before
and after seeding. First true leaf was the general harvest stage, with a
reported gain after delaying ‘Gem’ marigold roughly 1–2 days. The system was a
greenhouse with natural day length. Its crop results depend on variety, stage
and growing conditions; the shelf-day weights in the guide are illustrative.
Preservation: source_docs/supplier/Johnnys-2017-microgreens-yield-trial-29-varieties.pdf.

[T10] Deng et al., Sunflower and pea shoot microgreens, Horticulturae 7(11):446
URL: https://www.mdpi.com/2311-7524/7/11/446
Access: 2026-11-15; live. Class: primary microgreen study.
Used in: soaked seed drain check. Only seed water-retention measurements used.
Record: measured seed mass before and after water uptake in sunflower and pea.
The study did not optimize mechanical drain duration or moisture for 1020 coir.
Preservation: canonical open-access article and relevant method summary.

[T11] Tavan et al., Sensor-Based Irrigation Management in a Soilless Vertical Farm
URL: https://doi.org/10.3389/fsufs.2020.622720
Access: 2026-11-15; live. Class: primary microgreen trial.
Used in: paired water-uptake trial.
Record: kale on a soilless vertical farm, sensor-based versus gravimetric
irrigation at four water levels; sensor-based irrigation increased water use
efficiency by 13% overall. The 1020 capillary comparison does not transfer
the sensor thresholds or predict a similar gain.
Preservation: source_docs/studies/Tavan-2021-sensor-irrigation-kale-microgreens.pdf;
also available in Grow Lab’s sources directory.

[T12] University of Maryland Extension, Growing Microgreens and Baby Greens Indoors
URL: https://extension.umd.edu/resource/growing-microgreens-and-baby-greens-indoors
Access: 2026-11-15; live. Class: extension. Used in: cross-swap nested trays.
Record: documents the perforated insert over a solid tray for growing
microgreens. Cross-swapping pairs tests how seating affects the wetting pattern.
Preservation: URL and support notes; no full copy redistributed.

[T13] Both et al., Guidelines for measuring and reporting environmental
parameters for experiments in greenhouses. Plant Methods 11:43 (2015).
URL: https://doi.org/10.1186/s13007-015-0083-5
Access: 2026-11-15; live. Class: controlled environment methods.

[T14] Garrett Corwin / Piedmont Microgreens, Growing Sunflower Microgreens
URL: https://microgreenmanager.com/blog/growing-sunflower-microgreens
Access: 2026-11-15; live. Class: firsthand farm practice.
Used in: before-cut hull counts and timing.
Record: Corwin describes gently brushing open shoots across several days and
sorting at harvest. Cultivar, handling and quality specifications vary.
The 20-stem count and total-time comparison are local process checks.
Preservation: URL and support notes; local creator research at

[T15] Ghoora and Srividya, Packaging and Coating Technique and Postharvest Quality
URL: https://doi.org/10.3390/foods9050653
Access: 2026-11-15; live. Class: primary microgreen packaging trial.
Used in: keep pack trial fill weight fixed.
Record: radish and roselle, 15 g fill, two macro-perforated formats (PET
clamshell vs LDPE bag), 5°C experimental storage. The clamshell preserved
quality better in that comparison. This provides a design principle for
comparing packages, with no transfer of a storage temperature or shelf-life claim.
Preservation: source_docs/studies/Radish-roselle-packaging-postharvest-2020.pdf.

[T16] FDA, Refrigerator Thermometers — Cold Facts about Food Safety
URL: https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/refrigerator-thermometers-cold-facts-about-food-safety
Access: 2026-11-15; live. Class: official food-handling guidance.
Used in: cold shelf under normal harvest load.
Record: use an independent appliance thermometer; keep refrigerator at or
below 40°F. The two-position loaded-shelf check is a local implementation.
Preservation: URL and notes; related FDA produce guidance is archived at
source_docs/regulatory/food_safety/FDA-selecting-serving-produce-safely-2026-10-29.html.

[T20] Transport Canada, Improper mixing of common household cleaning products
