04 / BETWEEN EVERY CROP↗

Clean & sanitize trays

Reset every tray before planting again.

  1. Empty roots and spent medium.
  2. Wash, then rinse off the soap.
  3. Sanitize for the full wet contact time.
  4. Rinse if the label requires; air-dry.

Dollar Tree or store-brand bleach? Use the label checklist. See a small Clorox option, dilution and rinse rules.
