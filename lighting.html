The distance that matters

Measure from the leaves to the LED face.

Clearance = shelf pitch − fixture drop − medium-top height − shoot height

At a 14-inch pitch, a 1.5-inch fixture drop and medium top 1.5 inches above the lower shelf, 1-inch shoots leave 10 inches; 3-inch shoots leave 8; 5-inch shoots leave 6. At a 10-inch pitch, the same 5-inch shoots leave just 2 inches. These are dimensional examples, not measurements of a creator’s rack.

Trial roughly 6–10 inches of canopy clearance for modest-power bars, starting around 8 inches. Re-map as the crop rises. Closer can raise intensity directly below bars and worsen evenness; farther may improve overlap but lose more light beyond the shelf. A 4-foot bar is an extended source, so do not apply a whole-fixture inverse-square shortcut.

For tall pea or sunflower shoots, allow a taller pitch if the actual crop needs it: with the two 1.5-inch offsets, 5-inch shoots need 14–18 inches of pitch to keep a 6–10-inch gap. A shorter crop can use tighter shelves if measured PPFD, leaf temperature and form are satisfactory. Follow any manufacturer minimum clearance.

Commission one shelf before copying it

Let the edge readings choose the next step.

  1. Set the timer to 16 hours on, 8 off after the chosen covered germination stage.
  2. Measure at canopy height across each tray: center, edges and corners. A 3 × 3 grid per tray is a useful beginning. Record the layout and actual gap.
  3. Calculate mean PPFD, DLI, minimum and maximum. A center reading alone does not describe the shelf.
  4. If edges are weak but the center is adequate, reposition bars, improve overlap or add a distributed bar. Lowering everything can make hotspots worse.
  5. If the whole occupied area is low, trial a closer position, another bar or a fixture with documented higher PPF. Keep 16 hours fixed for this comparison.
  6. Check again near harvest. Compare saleable grams, stem length, color, losses and lighting kWh across repeated crop batches before scaling.

A phone reading can help with relative checks; verify device/spectrum response and any required diffuser. A calibrated quantum sensor gives a stronger basis for absolute PPFD. Daylight is additional and variable: this calculator assumes steady electric lighting and excludes it. [L02]

How the estimate works

One typical canopy value, from the whole planted area.

The automatic dose uses the median of equal-area samples across every tray: half the sampled canopy receives less modeled light, half more. It is not the midpoint of the minimum and maximum. The heatmap and dose use the same sampled field; colors show relative spread, while the estimate supplies an approximate absolute scale.

Your Barrina T8 preset uses 137.5 lumens per electrical watt from the supplied listing (5,500 lm ÷ 40 W). Other presets retain the previous 100 lm/W assumption. You can edit this under Fixture, trays & rack costs. Iowa State Extension’s rough 60 lumens per µmol/s conversion still supplies the photon estimate. Each bar is divided into 48 equal downward Lambertian emitters; the V-shaped T8’s real beam is not calibrated by this model. Geometry determines how much modeled light arrives at each canopy sample. [L29] [L28]

Actual spectrum, diffuser, side emission, reflectors, age and fixture efficiency can change the result substantially. Source conversion factors alone can vary from 40 to 80 lm per µmol/s: this gives a sensitivity range of 0.75–1.5 times the central estimate, not a confidence interval or a bound on total model error. No extra utilization multiplier is applied because geometric spill is already modeled. No daylight or reflections are added. Use a quantum/PAR reading to replace the estimate when available.

DLI = selected PPFD × hours/day × 0.0036; total = DLI × lit days. Auto projects the median sample’s dose at the displayed representative height. A spatial median is not an area mean and can hide weak corners; compare the displayed spread as well. The same PPFD is held through the period; this is not an integrated crop-growth simulation. The reference status remains a research comparison, not a crop pass/fail verdict. A measured custom value is cleared when physical geometry changes.
