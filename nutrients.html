04 / The “ocean water” question

How close is your MaxiGro mix?

You likely mean OceanSolution 2-0-3, a formulated liquid fertilizer. On The Grow favors it at 0.5 US fl oz per US gallon—about 3.906 mL/L. That is firsthand grower guidance, not proof of a universal winner.

The 0.80 g/L default gives 80 mg/L N and 112 mg/L K₂O. At an assumed OceanSolution density of 1.00 g/mL, its OTG mix gives about 78.1 and 117.2 mg/L. Nitrogen is 2.4% higher; potash is 4.4% lower. Actual density is unverified, and the remaining chemistry differs.
