The actual recommended rack: shelf pitch to leaf clearance

October 30, 2026. Product specifications, creator choices and installation measurements are different evidence layers. This model makes each explicit. Open the rack model.

Your default: a 24-inch shelf with contained trays

The model now opens with a 48 × 24-inch planning shelf, following your no-overhang requirement. The original 18-inch Costco model is available only as an explicit creator reference; it is not presented as the dimensions of your chosen 24-inch rack. Vertical dimensions start from the source family and require verification for the actual deeper rack you select. The original reference's four-tray layout fails the no-overhang check and cannot transfer to the lighting planner. A transferred 24-inch plan preserves that depth.

The MGM two-bar lighting method is adapted to the deeper planning shelf: assumed 2-inch edge insets mean 20 inches between bar centers, rather than the 14-inch reconstruction of the original 18-inch reference. These are separate layouts, not a revised claim about his physical rack.

Product identity and what is confirmed

Microgreen Manager’s rack link resolves to Costco item 1242823, titled TRINITY 6-Tier Wire Shelving Rack, 48 × 18 × 72 in, with wheels. The matching manufacturer EcoStorage TBFZ-0945 family gives six 48 × 18 shelves, overall 72 in on feet / 77 in on wheels, and one-inch shelf adjustment increments. The retail page does not expose a model/revision that proves every family part is identical to the creator’s installed unit. [L03,L24]

The official manufacturer manual shows shelves seated on split slip sleeves on grooved poles. It does not dimension the shelf rail’s vertical depth, total wire-grid thickness, precise deck-to-groove offset or the creator’s bottom shelf elevation. The linked current manual file is an older 2017 revision, so it supports construction principles and family identification, not a measured 2026 installation. [L25]

The light link is the Hykolity SLB-1141 family: published body dimensions 46.1 × 0.92 × 1.53 in. The 1.53-inch height is used as a provisional fixture-top-to-emitting-face distance; lens position and clip orientation should be verified on the actual fixture. [L11]

The linked Bootstrap Farmer shallow 1020 family publishes 21 × 10¾ × 1¼ in outer dimensions, 19¾ × 9½ in inner bottom and 1.8–2.0 mm material thickness. These are single-tray specifications. The website’s older light-spread diagram keeps its explicitly illustrative 21.25-inch rim example; it is not used to derive the new vertical gap. The current manufacturer’s 21-inch length would overhang an 18-inch shelf by 1.5 inches on each depth edge if centered. [L26]

Why rack height does not give shelf spacing

There are six shelf decks and five intervening bays. The top deck can hold storage and carry the lights for the bay below. Do not divide 72 or 77 inches by five and assume that is his shelf pitch. He adjusts the shelves; his written method reports roughly 10 inches between shelves. [L03,L08]

The older method also mentions 11 notches. Counting 11 groove marks including both endpoints spans 10 one-inch intervals, but the wording does not establish how he counted or which shelf surface he measured. The model therefore starts with 10-inch equal-point deck-to-deck pitch, not a supposedly measured 11-inch pitch or 10-inch clear opening. Identically seated shelves preserve the groove-spacing difference even though the absolute deck-to-groove offset is unknown.

Each of the five bay pitches can be changed independently in whole-inch mounting steps. Measured-pitch mode accepts fractional deck-to-deck distances (0.01-inch entry steps) for the actual seated or deflected shelf surfaces; this does not alter the hardware’s one-inch groove spacing. The default lowest deck is 8 inches above the feet datum, an exposed reconstruction, not a published MGM measurement. On wheels, the model adds an effective 5-inch overall-height difference to all floor elevations. That shift cancels from every leaf-to-light gap; the 4-inch caster wheel diameter is not substituted for its total installed lift.

With those defaults, decks are 8, 18, 28, 38, 48, 58 inches in the feet reference, or 13, 23, 33, 43, 53, 63 inches above floor in the wheels reference. These floor heights are conditional examples. The model checks whether the top deck exceeds the nominal frame extent; it is not a structural or stability analysis.

The light can sit inside the rail cavity

Shelf deck/grid and the lower edge of the perimeter rail are separate surfaces. A light zip-tied against the wire grid does not necessarily hang below the full rail depth. Deducting the entire shelf rail and then the whole fixture can exaggerate the light drop.

Three mounting modes are provided:

  • Against grid: drop = deck-top-to-grid-underside + tie/clip gap + fixture height.
  • Below rail: drop = deck-top-to-rail-underside + suspension gap + fixture height.
  • Measured: enter the actual upper deck top to LED emitting-face distance. This replaces the component sum.

The default grid/tie dimensions 0.125 + 0.125 in are explicitly assumed. The published light body height 1.53 in is added, yielding an illustrative 1.78-inch deck-to-LED drop. The default rail depth 1.5 in is unpublished and editable. It affects the rail clear opening, but does not enter the grid-mount drop.

On the same 10-inch pitch, a 1.5-inch rail creates an 8.5-inch rail opening. Mounting below that rail with 0.125-inch gap plus 1.53-inch fixture instead gives a 3.155-inch light drop. Those are different quantities and mounting configurations.

Nested tray height is not twice a tray’s depth

Two nested 1¼-inch trays do not simply create 2½ inches of elevation. Model the outer base, the spacing between nested bottoms, the inner base and the actual medium fill:

medium-top elevation = outer bottom thickness + nesting gap + inner bottom thickness + settled fill depth

Default 0.075-inch base thickness is a rounded proxy for 1.9 mm, the midpoint of the published material range. Actual bottom ribs and feet can differ from nominal wall thickness. The 0.125-inch nesting gap and 1-inch medium fill are unmeasured assumptions. These values produce:

0.075 + 0.125 + 0.075 + 1.000 = 1.275 inches above the lower deck

The modeled inner tray rim is 1.45 inches above the deck, which is a separate result from medium height. The model flags fill exceeding the available inner tray depth. Alternatively, use measured mode and enter the actual lower deck to medium-top elevation directly. [L26]

Resulting leaf-to-light distances

leaf gap = deck-to-deck pitch − deck-to-LED drop − medium elevation − shoot height above medium

Using the grid-mounted default 1.78-inch drop, 1.275-inch medium elevation and 10-inch pitch:

Shoot height Calculated gap
1 in 5.945 in, displayed about 5.95 in
3 in 3.945 in, displayed about 3.95 in
5 in 1.945 in, displayed about 1.95 in

These are conditional dimensional calculations, not measured MGM clearances. For a desired 6-inch gap over 3-inch shoots, these inputs need 12.055 inches of pitch; the next whole-inch shelf setting is 13 inches. A 12-inch pitch is about 0.055 inch short in this calculation—much smaller than the uncertainty in the assumed mounting/fill dimensions. Do not treat the final decimal as an installation tolerance or a biological threshold.

The model shows both full-five-lit-bay and older three-upper-lit-bay arrangements. Beginner germination bays have no LED clearance result and cannot be transferred as lit shelves. It also flags canopy/light intersection and a shelf layout extending beyond the frame.

Transfer to the light-spread and dose planner

The selected lit bay transfers its pitch, deck-to-LED drop, medium elevation, shoot height and number of lit levels through an explicit link. The lighting planner keeps the component precision (0.005-inch input steps for the offsets) so a 1.275-inch medium elevation is not silently rounded to an old 0.25-inch slider increment. Its optical map uses the transferred leaf-to-light gap. This is a geometry update, not a PPFD calibration.

The MGM preset in the main planner now uses the detailed default 1.78/1.275-inch offsets rather than the generic 1.5/1.5-inch examples. Other presets retain their labeled generic offsets. Daily/cumulative dose uses the default assumed 200 PPFD or another dropdown scenario; a measured canopy reading can optionally replace it. The dropdown is not calibrated from the rack geometry.

The four measurements that make it accurate on your installed rack

  1. Measure from the top of the lower wire deck to the same reference on the upper deck: actual pitch.
  2. Measure down from the upper deck top to the LED emitting face: actual lamp drop.
  3. Measure up from the lower deck to the settled medium surface: actual medium elevation.
  4. Measure from that surface to the top of the shoots: actual shoot height.

The measured modes remove the uncertain mounting/nesting decomposition. Shelf bending, tray bowing, uneven fill and uneven canopy can still create local variation; measure the relevant positions. Manufacturer dimensions alone cannot establish those installation details or a whole-shelf PPFD map.

Current shoot-height starting value

The rack page starts at 1.5-inch shoots, matching the single height slider in all lighting presets. This is a representative mid-growth planning example, not a measured crop average. With the unchanged 10-inch pitch, 1.78-inch lamp drop and 1.275-inch medium elevation, canopy clearance is 5.445 inches. Adjust the height for the actual crop. The detailed rack retains its 24-inch no-overhang adaptation as its starting footprint; the lighting page defaults to two 40 W Barrina T8 shop lights on the retained 18-inch layout. Imported rack bays use those T8 output settings; measure the installed drop for the actual fixture.
