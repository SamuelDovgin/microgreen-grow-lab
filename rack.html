What the product data can establish

Costco item 1242823 is the rack reached by MGM’s link. TRINITY TBFZ-0945 family specifications give six 48 × 18 shelves, 72″ on feet, 77″ on wheels and 1″ adjustment increments. The manufacturer manual shows slip sleeves and seated shelf assemblies; it does not provide the missing vertical installation dimensions. [L24–L25]

Hykolity SLB-1141 publishes 1.53″ body height. Bootstrap Farmer shallow trays publish 1¼″ depth and 1.8–2.0 mm material thickness. Neither figure establishes a mounted LED-face height or the elevation of a nested, filled tray pair. [L11,L26]

Accuracy limit: shelf rail/grid depth, clip/tie gap, nesting stand-off and fill depth are not measured from MGM’s actual rack. The displayed gap is conditional on the selected inputs. For an accurate installed result, measure deck-to-deck pitch, upper deck-to-LED face, lower deck-to-medium top and shoot height; use the measured modes above.
